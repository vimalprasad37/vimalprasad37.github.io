FAQ

Common questions

What Odoo versions do you work with?

Odoo 12 through 19, Community and Enterprise. Most new builds today go on 18 or 19, and I regularly upgrade older instances to them.

Do you work with clients in other time zones?

Yes — I work with clients across Europe, the US, the Middle East and Asia. I keep a few overlapping hours with your working day and agree a fixed communication window up front.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Happy to sign an NDA before any details are shared. Client code and data stay private, and nothing appears in my portfolio without written permission.

What happens after the project is delivered?

Every fixed-scope project includes 30 days of free bug support after go-live. Beyond that you can move to a monthly retainer or simply pay hourly when something comes up.

Can you take over an existing Odoo project?

Yes, and it is a large part of what I do. I start with a code and configuration audit, give you an honest assessment of the current state, then propose a plan to stabilise or continue it.

How are payments structured?

Fixed projects are split across milestones — typically an advance to start, then payments as each milestone is accepted. Hourly and retainer work is invoiced monthly.

Do you provide documentation and training?

Yes. Technical documentation is included with every build, and I run walkthrough sessions for your team so you are not dependent on me for day-to-day use.
